# Money: the payments inbox and the ledger

<!-- Moved word for word from the root CLAUDE.md "Payments inbox / The ledger" section on 2026-11-08 (it was over the 150k-character limit). The root keeps the always-follow rules; this file loads when Claude touches a matching path. -->

### Payments inbox (e-transfer auto-detection)
`docs/plans/payments.md`, flag `NEXT_PUBLIC_FLAG_PAYMENTS_AUTO` (server-read). The admin's Gmail runs `public/payments/apps-script.gs`, which forwards each Interac notification to `POST /api/payments/etransfer` with `x-payments-key` — a per-club secret (`<groupId>.<hex>`) minted in the admin card and stored as a sha256 in the club's `clubSettings` doc (`paymentsSettingsId(g)`). `lib/paymentsInbox.ts` owns ingest, the key, assign and the soft hold; `lib/etransferParse.ts` and `lib/etransferMatch.ts` are pure.
- **Never guess with money.** Auto-match needs an AUTHENTICATED email (the topmost, `mx.google.com`-stamped `Authentication-Results` shows `dkim=pass` for an `interac.ca` domain AND the From is `interac.ca`) ADDRESSED TO THE CLUB (its To names the script's own Gmail or the club's pay-to address, `addressedElsewhere`; DKIM proves Interac wrote an email, not whose money it was, so a member could re-deliver the notice of a transfer to someone else. A script copy older than 2026-11-05 sends no To and is trusted as before — paste the new one), exactly one candidate person, and an amount equal to one owed line, the oldest k lines, or the whole balance. Anything else is a `review` row in the admin's "Needs a look" list. An unauthenticated email still carries its proposed match, so the admin's tap is a confirmation — after checking the bank. **And only a SETTLED line is ever paid without a person looking** (`settledLines` in `lib/owedBalance.ts`, 2026-10-21): an unsettled past session's live share is what Home shows, but it moves until settle, and a row paid at the estimate was then stamped a different `owedAmount` with `paid: true` already on it, so the difference was never collected and never shown. "Pay with credit" reads the same list; an admin assigning a queued payment by ref may still pick any line.
- **Candidates are what Home shows** — `lib/owedBalance.ts` `computeOwed` is shared with `GET /api/players/unpaid`, sessions AND finished stringing jobs, so a player who sends their Home total matches.
- **Three clues name the person**: the sender's LEGAL name through `aliases` (etransferName → appName), the sender name as a roster name, and the MEMO's tail when it is exactly a roster name (the receipt template `{group} {date} - {name}` puts it there — verified on a real notification). Clues naming two different people are a review, never a guess. The queue's "Remember X as Y" writes the alias.
- **Dedupe is Interac's `Reference Number`** when an authenticated email carries one, else the Message-ID.
- **`Player.paid` stays the truth.** A match writes `paid: true` plus additive `paidAt` / `paidVia: 'etransfer'` / `paymentId`; the admin's manual tap writes `paidVia: 'manual'`. A row already paid or covered when a payment lands is skipped, never overwritten.
- **The email body is never stored**; `payments` docs are anonymized by `purgeMember`.
- **The soft hold is OFF by default** (`holdAfterUnpaid` 0 in the club's payments settings doc); the admin card shows who it would hold before it is switched on. On, a member owing for ≥ N SETTLED sessions — counted by SESSION, so a plus-one's line on the same night is not a second one, and not counting lines they self-reported — signs up onto the waitlist with `heldForUnpaid: true`; a payment (inbox or manual) releases it, and promotes them only into a spot no earlier waitlister is waiting for. Admin-added sign-ups bypass it, and it fails OPEN on a read error.
- **Reminders** (`lib/paymentReminders.ts`, OFF by default): the club's Gmail script calls `POST /api/payments/remind` daily with the same key — App Service runs nothing on a schedule, and the script already holds the key. A push at settle +3 and +7 days, then silence; settled lines only, never self-reported, never older than 30 days; one push per person; lines stamped in `Player.remindedAt` BEFORE the send, so a crashed run can miss a nudge but never double one. **The run, the card's "would remind" preview and its "would hold" list all come from ONE `computeOwedForRoster` read** (`dueRemindersFrom` / `wouldHoldFrom` are pure over it, 2026-10-21): each used to walk the roster through the per-person `computeOwed` — about four serial round trips a member, twice, on every Command Center open and every payment tap — and `__tests__/payment-reminders.test.ts` pins that `GET /api/admin/payments` never calls the per-person path. **No amount in the body** (lock-screen rule).
- **What a member has PAID is read from the rows** (`GET /api/members/me/payments`, `lib/memberPayments.ts`, Profile → Account balance's chart, 2026-11-09): their own paid `players` rows (`owedAmount`, in the month of `paidAt`, else `settledAt`, else the session's date), their guests' shares (`guestOf`) and their paid `stringingJobs`, through the same player-first reads as the balance (`loadOwedInputs`). Not the ledger, until a reconcile has run clean. A paid row with no frozen `owedAmount` (paid at an estimate, never settled) adds nothing rather than a guess. Always the caller's own, in the club their cookie names (`__tests__/group-member-payments.test.ts`).
- **The pay-to address reaches a member in one place**: `payTo` on `GET /api/players/unpaid` (`lib/payTo.ts`), only when they owe — security rule 10 keeps it off every general read.

### The ledger (store credit, gift cards, and the Phase 2 mirror)
`docs/plans/payments.md`. Container `ledger` (PK `/memberId`, GROUP scoped) is **append-only** — never upsert, edit or delete; a reversal is `void:<idBeingReversed>` (exact negation, keyed on the id it reverses so a double void 409s). **Sums are by `account`, never by partition or kind-list**: a member's partition holds both `member_owed` (charges +, payments/covers −) and `member_credit`; `club_outlay` lives under the `~club` sentinel (court cost per settled session from the snapshot, shuttle purchases, expenses). `sumAccount` / `balanceOf` in `lib/storeCredit.ts`; entries without `account` are credit. **A LISTED credit history is `creditLedgerFor`, never `ledgerFor`** — the balance was narrowed to credit on day one, but the two histories (`GET /api/credit`, `GET /api/admin/credit`) read the whole partition and would have shown charges and voids as "recent credit" the moment the mirror flag went on (review of #562). **Ids are derived, never minted** (`charge:<row>:<ms(settledAt)>`, `payment:<row>:<paymentId|ms(paidAt)|legacy>`, `cover:<row>:<ms>:<n>`, stringing `charge:<job>:<n>` and `payment:<job>:<ms(paidAt)>`, `court:<session>:<ms>`, `shuttles:<purchase>`) so a live write, the backfill and a repair compute the same id and the second 409s. **A stringing payment is keyed on `paidAt` whatever the `via`** — a `StringingJob` has no `paidVia`/`paymentId` (a `Player` does), so the backfill cannot tell how a job was paid; the first cut keyed an e-transfer on its `paymentId` and a job paid through the inbox between deploy and backfill would have been mirrored twice (review of #562). **A PAID job cannot be re-priced** (`PATCH /api/stringing/jobs/[id]` answers 409 `job_paid` to a differing `priceCents`, with `force` or as a proposal, 2026-10-21; and since 2026-11-05 so does the player's `accept` of a proposal made before the payment — that route also mirrors an accepted re-price now, which it never did): the payment entry's id is derived from `paidAt` at the price paid, so re-pricing underneath it would leave the job's owed account netting to new − old while the row says paid, and the reconcile would report it daily with no repair (the backfill computes the same ids); the path is unpaid → re-price → paid (a new `paidAt`, so a new payment id at the new amount), and `paid: false` in the same body is accepted because the mirror reads prev against next and sees both transitions. **A hook that writes a DELTA must first check the base exists**: between deploy (flag on) and the backfill, a row the mirror never saw can be edited, and `mirrorShuttleAdjusted` once wrote `+$20` for a purchase whose `shuttles:<id>` entry did not exist yet — the backfill then wrote the base from the CURRENT cost and the club was out $140 on a $120 purchase (review of #562); with no base it writes the base from `next` and no delta. **`lib/ledgerMirror.ts` never throws** — every hook is called AFTER the primary write, on a TRANSITION only, swallows and counts failures (`getMirrorFailures`), and is a no-op with `NEXT_PUBLIC_FLAG_LEDGER_MIRROR` off; the rows (`Player.paid`, `owedAmount`, `StringingJob.paidAt`) stay the truth until a reconcile has run clean. `lib/ledgerBackfill.ts` writes history with the same builders (`POST /api/admin/ledger-backfill`, the `migrate-groups` key gate, bounded, resumable by `remaining`). **The reconcile (`lib/ledgerReconcile.ts`, stage 2) compares FROZEN money only**: the settled rows and billable stringing jobs Home shows a person (`computeOwedForRoster` in `lib/owedBalance.ts` — the whole-roster read `lib/owedRows.ts` moved the per-person path AWAY from, fine once a day) against the ledger's open `member_owed` balance per `ref.id`; an unsettled session's live estimate is left out because the ledger holds nothing to compare it with. A line nobody on the roster is shown (an unlinked legacy row, a purged member's `~anon` charge, a REMOVED member still owing) takes the ROW as its live side and is counted as `unlisted`, not flagged — the review of #570 caught the removed-member case, which would have turned the card amber daily over a line with no repair. Four codes (`missing_charge`, `missing_payment`, `stale_charge`, `amount_mismatch`), **no auto-repair** (the repair is the backfill again, or the admin redoing the action — a reconcile that wrote entries would be a second mirror with its own bugs). It runs on the daily `POST /api/payments/remind` call BEFORE the `remindersOn` check and never fails that run; the outcome lives on the club's payments settings doc as ids and cents (never names); `GET`/`POST /api/admin/ledger/reconcile` names the rows for the admin and the E-transfers card warns on a mismatch or a check older than 2.5 days. **The money view is `GET /api/admin/ledger` (stage 3)** — `lib/ledgerView.ts` `summarizeLedger` buckets the club's ledger in JS (the mock ignores a date parameter it does not know): income, collected by method (an e-transfer is `etransferAuto` only when its `payments` doc says `matchedBy: 'auto'`), covered, outlay by category, net, and credit liability (positive balances only, never windowed). **A void is dated by the entry it reverses**, or a March charge unsettled in May would net inside May and leave March standing. **Two sources, never one number**: `outstanding` is LIVE from `computeOwedForRoster` (frozen lines, everyone, not range-bound) and `unfinalized` is the live `costSplit` estimate for unsettled sessions under its OWN key, last on the page so it can never read as a total. **All or nothing**: any read that throws is a 503 `read_failed` (`read-failed-503.test.ts`) — six true buckets and one silent zero is the lying empty state with a straight face. Admin-entered expenses are `POST`/`GET`/`DELETE /api/admin/expenses` (`expense:<random>` under `~club`, dated by `meta.date`; delete is a void). Sentinel ids (`~club`, `~unlinked`, `~anon`) can't collide with `randomBytes` member ids; `purgeMember` refuses them and **re-keys a purged member's `member_owed` entries under `~anon`** (the club's totals need them) while deleting their credit. The mock ignores partition keys: a `gift:<hash>` ledger entry alone would let two members redeem one card, which is why a gift card is claimed by a `clubSettings` doc first.

**Store credit** (`lib/storeCredit.ts`, flag `NEXT_PUBLIC_FLAG_STORE_CREDIT`): **A gift card's record is a READING of the redeemer's credit, never a second balance** (`docs/plans/gift-card-ledger.md`, 2026-10-09): `attributeCredit` in `lib/creditAttribution.ts` draws every spend from the oldest source still holding something, a refund gives its spend back to the sources it came from, and `giftCardRecords` resolves an old card's redeemer from its `gift:<hash>` entry while a new one carries `redeemedBy`. **Giving a card is an ORGANISER's** (Grant, 2026-11-10: "gift card should be organizer feature only"): `/api/admin/giftcards` (the console's mint and every card's record) and `/api/giftcards` (Profile's "Give a gift card") both answer `organiser_only` to anyone else, the rank re-read on every call; with groups off every admin is one. The `canGift` switch that handed minting to a helper is retired: nothing reads it and `PATCH /api/members` no longer writes it (`__tests__/group-giftcards.test.ts`). Redeeming is every member's. **Idempotence is in the ids — and Cosmos ids are unique only WITHIN a partition.** A gift card is claimed by a `clubSettings` doc `<giftDocId>:claim` (PK `/id`, so unique outright); a `gift:<hash>` ledger entry alone would let two DIFFERENT members both redeem one card, since the ledger is partitioned by member — and the mock, ignoring partitions, would pass that test. A spend is `spend:<lineRef>` in the spender's own partition (a double tap 409s), and re-reads the balance after inserting, refunding itself if a concurrent spend took it below zero. Gift codes are stored as `clubSettings` docs keyed by the code's hash (`giftDocId`) — the plaintext exists only in the mint response — and unknown/used/malformed all answer one `gift_not_found`. Credit pays a line only when it covers it IN FULL and only a SETTLED one (`payWithCredit`, `settledLines`, oldest first, `paidVia: 'credit'`); if the row moved under it, a `credit_refund` entry returns the money. **A stale spend — written and never finished — is reconciled against the ROW on the next tap or balance read, whatever happened to the row** (`refundStaleSpends`, 2026-10-21): a COMPLETED payment (the common case, forever) is answered by the mirror's `payment` entry with `meta.via: 'credit'` and NO row read; otherwise the row is read: still unpaid → finished; paid another way, covered, removed or gone → `refund:<ref>:<n>`. It walks the LEDGER, not the owed lines, because a row paid by e-transfer is no longer owed and the old owed-line walk never visited it. Account deletion deletes the member's ledger (credit is forfeited).

## Gotchas

<!-- Moved word for word from the root CLAUDE.md "Gotchas" on 2026-11-09, to keep the always-loaded file short. -->

- **Previous session snapshot**: `session.prevSessionDate` and `session.prevCostPerPerson` are frozen at advance time. `<PrevPaymentReminder />` renders below the sign-up card, gated on `hasIdentity` (not `isSignedUp`) — so players who played last week still see what they owe even before signing up for the next session (research finding 4.8). `hasIdentity` must be kept in sync with `setIdentity` / `clearIdentity` call sites. Not live-updated if the archived session is edited later.
- **Cover modes (`Player.coverMode`)**: a covered (`writtenOff:true`) player is `'absorb'` (admin eats their share; they stay in the per-person denominator) or `'resplit'` (excluded from the denominator → spread across the other payers). Legacy `writtenOff` with no `coverMode` reads as `'absorb'`. Settle (`/api/session/settle`) is the single calculator and stamps `SettledSnapshot.coveredTotal` (Σ per-person over absorb-covered). `CoverSheet` re-freezes the bill when the session was already settled so amounts update immediately — through `lib/resettleSession.ts`, the ONE owner, shared with `PaymentsCard`'s uncover. **Since 2026-11-05 that is ONE request, `POST /api/session/settle { resettle: true }`, and it keeps EVERYONE the frozen bill billed** (`__tests__/settle-resettle-keeps-billed.test.ts`): it was DELETE then POST from the browser, the POST billed only rows still on the list, and so "Cover & remove" (the club absorbs) and "Remove without covering" (they still owe) both put the removed person's share on everyone else and wiped their debt. A row removed after its bill froze is still a participant, still owes (credit may pay it), and still "attended" in a member's history. A change that would move what someone ALREADY PAID is refused before any write — the re-settle answers 409 `paid_would_change` with the names, and `PATCH /api/players` refuses a resplit cover (or uncover) on a settled bill anyone has paid — because a paid row re-stamped at a new amount keeps `paid: true` over a figure nobody paid. A manual unsettle (DELETE) still forgets the bill. Surfaced in `PaymentsCard`'s per-player menu (unconditional since `NEXT_PUBLIC_FLAG_LEDGER` retired 2026-08-29).
- **The Payments card shows one session at a time** (`docs/plans/payments-today-first.md`, 2026-10-21): ‹ › step to the neighbouring sessions and the date opens `SessionDatePicker`, a month calendar where only session days are buttons and each carries its money as a dot (amber owing, grey all paid, green the current session); ‹ carries an amber dot while an earlier month still owes. It replaced a grid of every session as a chip. The dots read `GET /api/sessions/history?counts=1` — counts only, no receipts, up to 200 sessions — because the receipt list stops at 24 and an older debt then had no mark at all. A session the counts never answered for gets no dot, never "all paid". The rules are `lib/sessionCalendar.ts`, and so is the cap: `SESSION_CALENDAR_LIMIT` bounds both that `counts=1` read and `GET /api/sessions` (the picker's list, projected to the nine fields the card and `AssignUsageSheet` read — never a `SELECT *` over the history again), so the list and its dots stop at the same session.
- **`lib/buildReceiptInput.ts` is the single cost-per-person + receipt resolver**: computes `costPerPerson` once and returns a ready `ReceiptInput` — snapshot-first for settled sessions (the cover-aware `SettledSnapshot`), best-effort recompute via `sessionCostTotals` otherwise. Drives `GET /api/sessions/history`, `PastSessionsPage`, and `PaymentsCard`'s summary header + receipts, so a list amount and its receipt can't diverge. Don't hand-roll a fourth cost computation (settle, `members/[id]/history`, and this each grew their own). **The denominator itself is `costSplit` in `lib/sessionCost.ts`**: settle freezes what it returns, `buildReceiptInput` and the history route call it for an unsettled session and take `session.settled` for a settled one, and `CommandCenter` calls `buildReceiptInput`. **The LIVE estimates go through it too since 2026-10-21**: `classifyOwed`'s ctx carries the session's ROWS (`roster`, from `loadOwedInputs`' one projected read of the unsettled sessions — two more fields, no extra query) and the advance route's `prevCostPerPerson` fallback calls `costSplit` over every row of the current session, so Home's balance line, the advance snapshot and the receipt cannot disagree over a cover resplit before settle; a count cannot carry a `resplit`, which is why the ctx is rows and not a number (`__tests__/live-estimate-one-rule.test.ts`). `PaymentsCard`/`PastSessionsPage` own their OWN `<ReceiptSheet>` because `CommandCenter.openReceipt` fetches the ACTIVE session only and can't render a past session's receipt.
