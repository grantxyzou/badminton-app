# Foldables

<!-- Moved word for word from `.claude/rules/design-system.md` on 2026-11-09, to keep that file under the 40k limit `__tests__/docs-canary.test.ts` enforces. The rules that hold for every edit (nothing crosses the centre, those blocks stay last, `--shell-pad-x`) stay in design-system.md as a short version. -->

- **Foldables get two panes; one screen gets exactly what it had** (`docs/plans/foldable-layout.md`, 2026-11-04). The last two `@media` blocks in `globals.css` — a touch screen ≥ 720 wide and ≥ 480 tall, or two side-by-side viewport segments — put the week in the RIGHT pane — the page that opens, Grant's reading of a foldable as a book (2026-11-04: "the elevation opening up more like a page"; Apple's Mail example has the primary list on the left, a convention and not a rule) — and the nudges and your account in the left; on Stats (2026-11-12, Grant: "go with 1") the overview (title, the Level / Games / Kudos tiles, then You's greeting — a shell child, `youLead`, not a register card) in the left and the register switch with the register's cards in the right (`.stats-shell*`, also on its loading frame); on Stringing (same day, Grant: "Option 2 but can we have something opened by default on the right?") the title, switch and register in the left and what a tap opens — a job, a string, your rackets — in the right, OPEN BY DEFAULT (the top job, the map's string, your rackets or else the shelf), through a slot the tab renders only when `usePaneMode()` says two pages (`components/stringing/StringingAside.tsx`; one screen gets no slot and its old page or sheet; the tab widens only while the slot shows something); Profile (Edit profile open by default) and Admin (Roster by default) the same list-detail, and the bench's own pages on the right too (all 2026-11-12, "finish the app with apple guideline") — a right page has no Back (`PaneDetail`, read by `TopBar`; a page opened from it is `nested` and keeps one), comes into view when it changes (`usePaneDetailReveal`), and opens with `.pane-page-open` (in from the fold, a `--space-5` glide under a fade; reduced motion keeps the fade); signed out as a book's spread (the launch lockup on the left page — the splash's too — and the doors or the Log in / Sign up form on the right, `.signed-out-cover`, `--launch-kh` from the pre-paint script); Notifications' settings left and Recent right; open sheets and toasts in the LEFT half (so they never cover the week they were raised from), and, when the screen is also landscape, move the tab bar to the right edge (Apple's "vertical controls"). The gap is in the EXACT centre, so the fold lands in it without detecting it, and **nothing but the page background and a bottom tab bar crosses it — never a banner** (Grant: "NEVER"). Those blocks stay LAST because they move rules defined earlier. Nothing outside them may change a single screen: side padding is `--shell-pad-x` (16px when the insets are 0). `__tests__/foldable-layout-canary.test.ts` pins all of it. **Use the `foldable-review` skill for any change that could land on an unfolded phone** (`.claude/skills/foldable-review/`): Apple's rules in Apple's words, this repo's decisions, and `scripts/fold-shots.mjs`, which screenshots and measures the app at the sizes the Duo and the Z Fold report with a fold injected — the suite cannot see any of this. **Where the fold IS is `lib/fold.ts`, the one owner of three sources** (step 3): the store app's `BpmFold` plugin (iOS reads `UIView.reservedRegions(kind: .division)` off the WebView — **`BpmFoldPlugin.swift` compiles only with Xcode 27.1+** — Android reads WindowManager's `FoldingFeature`), then the web Device Posture + Viewport Segments APIs, then nothing; **unknown is NOT flat** — `posture: null`, and `splitForFold` takes the viewport's centre. Its one consumer is the courtside scoreboard (`components/stats/CourtsideScoreboard.tsx`, "Keep score" on Stats → Play): a fixed overlay split at the fold, the score facing the court and the +1 buttons on the half in the hand, handed to Add a game prefilled on Save. Its digits are `--court-digit` (a `clamp()`), deliberately not a `--fs-*` rung because the type canary pins those to pixels. **Motion is the system's recipes** (same day): the fold/unfold reflow CROSSFADES through `lib/paneMode.ts` (`data-pane-flip` alternates `a`/`b` on `<html>`, two identical keyframes so the fade replays; opacity only, kept under reduced motion), the board's split transitions its grid tracks so it follows the hinge, opening is `.motion-fade`, each new digit and status line is keyed to fade in, and only the game ending takes `.status-celebrate`.
