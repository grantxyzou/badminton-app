# Stringing service

<!-- Moved word for word from the root CLAUDE.md "Gotchas" on 2026-11-09, to keep the always-loaded file short. -->

- **String stock is COUNTED from jobs, never logged** (`docs/plans/string-inventory.md`, 2026-10-10): `lib/stringStock.ts` `summarizeStringStock` takes purchases — `kind: 'stringPurchase'` rows in `clubSettings`, beside the gift cards, NOT a container of their own — and counts one set per job in `STRUNG_STATUSES`, matched to the offered list by label; a purchase mirrors to the ledger as kind `expense`, category `strings`, id `strings:<id>`. The offered list's `links` map (label → catalog id, additive on `OfferedStringsDoc`) is what gives a string its member-facing page (`StringsWeOfferCard`) and its reel length; a PATCH without `links` keeps the stored ones. **The first cut ensured a `stringStock` container on first touch and production answered the Bench "Couldn't load the string stock" the day it shipped** (2026-10-10): creating a container was the one thing that route did that nothing else in production does, and a shared-throughput Cosmos database refuses a 26th (`docs/azure.md` §3 — the code defined 27 and the portal count was never read). The likeliest cause, so the fix stops needing a container; the 503 now carries `step` naming the read that failed, and `ensureContainer` logs `[ensure-container]` on a refused create. **A new container is a known cost, not a free line in the registry** — count first.
- **The stringing notification seam has adapters on both sides now.** `lib/stringingNotify.ts` builds the notice; `lib/stringingNotifyDispatch.ts` sends it from `PATCH /api/stringing/jobs/[id]` when the status ACTUALLY moved. Two channels, and they are INDEPENDENT: **push runs ABOVE the email gate**, because the dispatcher used to return early on `no_email` and `Member.email` is only populated by the email/password and Google paths — wiring push in below that gate would have shipped a channel that reached almost nobody while every test stayed green. Push is the channel that works for a PIN-only member, which is most of them. **The push body carries no money at all** — not the exact figure and not the band. That is stricter than `toPlayerJob` on purpose: a banner renders on a locked phone, in a gym, in front of whoever is standing there, and "your racket is ready" is complete without a price. **A NEW REQUEST pushes too** (`lib/stringingRequestNotify.ts`, 2026-10-11, `docs/plans/stringing-audit-fixes.md`): every admin on the group's roster, minus the requester, gets "Lin's Astrox 88D Pro is waiting on the bench" — player and racket, no spec, no money — and an ASSIGNMENT pushes the assignee ("…is yours", `?tab=stringing`), never a self-claim. Not every `canString` member on the request: when that was decided a non-admin stringer's card showed only jobs assigned to them, so that push led to nothing. **A request NOBODY MOVES nudges the stringers** (`lib/stringingNudge.ts`, 2026-11-12, `docs/plans/stringing-request-nudge.md`): still at `requested` 48 hours after it was made, and again at 3 days, it pushes every `canString` member and organiser of the club (or only its stringer once someone has it), stamped in `requestNudgedAt` under the job's etag BEFORE the send so it never goes twice; the hourly tick (`lib/tick.ts`, `POST /api/cron/tick`, `.github/workflows/tick.yml`, `CRON_KEY`) runs it. A stringer's card lists those requests under "Waiting for a stringer" with Take it (`PATCH { memberId, claim: true }`, their own and nothing else), and any request past 48 hours wears `.bpm-job-card--waiting` on the bench and the card (`lib/stringingWaiting.ts`, one rule for all three). **The player hears about FORWARD moves only** — an undo (`ready` → `strung`) says nothing, where it used to send "being strung" right after "ready". Before that nothing told anyone a request existed; three unclaimed requests showed as "1 need you" on Profile, which now counts unclaimed and overdue jobs as a fourth signal (`benchSignals` in `lib/useAdminNeedsYou.ts`, shared with the Command Center's bench row, which leads the list with "N waiting · M late"). The same audit split `requested` out of `with_stringer` into its own player stage, `awaiting_dropoff` (a racket still in the bag is not "with" anybody; the name is deliberately not `requested`, because `stringing-rules` pins the two unions disjoint), folded the bench's set-up cards under a collapsed "Set-up" below Archived so the list comes first, and took "Grant" out of every player string — `docs/roles.md` strips the stringer on purpose, and a second club reads them too.
- **A player sees their own stringing price EXACTLY** (since 2026-10-28, `docs/plans/stringing-canvas-redesign.md`; Grant: "dont hide"). `toPlayerJob` sends `quote` (the stringer's figure, dollars) and, once the job is billable — finished, priced and unpaid (`lib/stringingBilling.ts`) — `amountDue`, the figure the balance card adds. It was a $4 band (`$28–32`) until then; `priceBand` is gone. Money on the stringing surfaces goes through `fmtMoney` in `lib/fmt.ts` (whole when whole, never rounded). The email quotes the figure; **the push still carries no money**, and a stringer who is not an admin still sees none (`toStringerJob`).
- **String stock is counted per COLOUR once a purchase carries one** (`docs/plans/string-stock-by-colour.md`, 2026-11-11). `StringPurchase.color` is an optional palette id (`lib/stringPalette.ts`) — absent is "Mixed / not sure" and every older purchase — in the same `clubSettings` row; no new container or kind. `summarizeStringStock` keeps two pools per line: one per tagged colour, one mixed. A strung job draws from its colour's pool when that colour was bought tagged (`colourIdOf` maps the job's free-text `stringColor` — the palette's English names, which the catalog shares — to an id), from the mixed pool otherwise. A tagged colour that runs dry takes its excess from the MIXED pool (the one pool that could have held it), never from another tagged colour, and what neither covers is `overdrawnSets`; the line's metres left are the SUM of its pools, so the colours can never add up to more than the total (`docs/plans/string-stock-audit-fixes.md`, 2026-11-15 — the first cut clamped each pool alone and 0 white + 20 mixed sat beside a total of 15). **Both job reads must project `c.stringColor`** (`app/api/stringing/stock/route.ts`, `lib/stringShelfRead.ts`), or every job counts as mixed. **The request sheet's colours come from `requestColours` in `lib/stringShelf.ts`, never the catalog directly**: the stringer's reel colours (`OfferedStringsDoc.colours`), else the maker's; narrowed, when any purchase is tagged, to `colourStock`'s in-stock ids plus the base list while the mixed pool has sets. Out of stock is HIDDEN (Grant: let the stringer's colour edits decide, "so this scenario will not be run into"). The public `colourStock` map says which colours, never a count. The sheet sends the palette's ENGLISH name as `stringColor` (the bench reads it) and shows the translated one.
- **A two-colour set and a club's own colour are reel colour ids like any palette one** (`docs/plans/string-stock-by-colour.md`, Decisions, 2026-11-12): `combo:<mains>+<crosses>` (English name `"<mains> / <crosses>"`, mains FIRST; `colourIdOf` also reads the catalog's `"Red/White"` as the set) and `custom:<rrggbb>` (the id is the hex; its name lives in `OfferedStringsDoc.customColours`, added only by an ADMIN's `addColours` on the strings PATCH, before the colour lists, all or none). So every colour path — reel lists, a purchase's `color`, the per-colour pools, the request sheet — takes them unchanged; a reader that names or counts a colour passes the club's `customColours` (`colourIdOf(name, customs)`, `summarizeStringStock({ customColours })`, `useReelColourName(customs)`), or a club colour reads as unknown. Both shelf sheets pick through ONE `components/stringing/ReelColourPicker.tsx`.
- **A stringer who is not an admin may rename a shelf string and set its reel colours** (`docs/plans/stringer-shelf-edits.md`, 2026-11-12; Grant: "open up reel and string name, colour for stringers"). `PATCH /api/stringing/strings` takes, from a live member cookie that `canStringIn` THIS club, exactly one of `{ rename }` or `{ colours: { <label>: ids } }` (merged into the stored map by `mergeColours`; an empty list clears it) — any other key, or both at once, is `403 forbidden_field`, never quietly dropped, the same rule the jobs route keeps for a stringer. The stringer's answer is `{ strings, colours }` only (no prices, links or aliases). The colours-only shape is open to an admin too. UI: `components/stringing/StringerShelfCard.tsx` under the stringer's queue on "Stringing jobs"; its sheet saves the rename FIRST and then the colours under the new name, with one Save in the footer.
- **A job can be for a WALK-UP, someone not on the roster** (`docs/plans/stringing-walk-ups.md`, 2026-11-12; Grant: "give me the ability to add a job for people thats not in the roster"). `POST /api/stringing/jobs { walkUp: true, memberName, walkUpContact? }` (admin only) ignores any body `memberId` and mints `~walkup:<16 hex>` as the job's partition key, one per job. The `~` makes it a ledger sentinel (`isSentinelLedgerId`), so purge and the credit route refuse it, and a MEMBER job with a `~` id is refused. `isWalkUpJob` in `lib/stringing.ts` is the one test. Nothing is PUSHED to a walk-up (`notifyPlayerOf*` return `reason: 'walk_up'` before any read); **one whose contact is an email gets two automatic emails** (Grant, 2026-11-12): the spec from `POST` after the create and a ready email from `PATCH` on the first FORWARD move to `ready`, through `lib/stringingWalkUpEmail.ts` (the member email's transport and sender, never throwing), each CLAIMED under the job's etag before the send (`{ at, ok: false, sending: true }`; the ready claim rides the status write) and stamped with the outcome after it in `walkUpEmails.{spec,ready}` — a sent one never repeats, a claim younger than a minute (`walkUpEmailInFlight`) holds every other send off, and admin-only `PATCH { resendSpecEmail: true }` resends only a failed or missing spec on a job that is not over (`walkUpJobClosed`). `walkUpEmailOf` is the one test. Any walk-up's job page shares a receipt image (`StringingReceiptSheet`, drawn in `lib/receiptTemplate.ts`) that names the person, never the contact. A `propose` on their job is applied at once, and a direct re-price needs no `force`, because nobody can confirm it in the app. `job_paid` still holds. `walkUpContact` reaches admins, and the assigned stringer through `toStringerJob(job, { contact: true })`, never the `unclaimed` list, a player or a push. Money uses the existing paths: no member's balance reads that id, the mirror writes its charge and payment under it, and the reconcile counts an unpaid one as `unlisted`.
- **A job counts against the shelf by more than its exact label, and a deleted job keeps its set used** (2026-11-12; Grant: "the stock is not going down based on the archived and finished stringing job"). Two causes, both fixed in `summarizeStringStock` / `lib/stringStock.ts`: (1) the bench fills a job's string from the player's BAG, where a catalog string is named "Yonex BG80", and stock matched only the shelf label "BG80" — so a job's label now tries, in order, the exact label, an alias, the catalog's `brand model` and `model` of a LINKED shelf string, then either with spaces and punctuation gone (`compactKey`), each only where it names exactly ONE shelf string (an ambiguous name stays in `unmatched`, never guessed); (2) deleting an archived job removed its usage — `DELETE /api/stringing/jobs/[id]` now writes a `kind: 'stringUse'` row in `clubSettings` (`su-<jobId>`, label, colour, day, nothing about the person or money) BEFORE removing a job in `STRUNG_STATUSES` (a failed write stops the delete), and both stock reads fold those rows in through `withDeletedUses`, which skips a row whose job still exists. Any new stock reader must read `readStringUses` too, or a deleted job's set comes back.
- **Stringing is a club's own switch, an add-on** (`docs/plans/stringing-add-on.md`, phase 1, 2026-11-13; Grant: "What if we tray the entire stringing feature as an add-on?"). `run.stringing: 'on' | 'off'` in `lib/clubRun.ts`, read through `clubRunOf`: a gap is on for `first_come`/`custom` (BPM) and off for `members_first`, a style never writes it and `styleOf` ignores it. It sits UNDER `NEXT_PUBLIC_FLAG_STRINGING`. **Off means there is nothing to show**: `PATCH /api/admin/settings` refuses to switch it off (409 `stringing_open_jobs`, with the count) while any job is on the bench or finished and unpaid (`isOpenStringingJob` in `lib/stringingService.ts`), so the pushes, nudge, balance and reconcile need no gate of their own. The server refuses only a NEW job (`POST /api/stringing/requests` and `POST /api/stringing/jobs` answer the flag-off 404 through `stringingServiceOnFor`, which fails open); reads and moves on existing jobs never check it. Screens hide on a KNOWN off only (`useStringingHidden`): three tabs (`BottomNav hideStringing`, indicator width `100% / --rn`), a Stringing deep link lands on Home, no bench row or count, no "Request stringing" on Stats or the app icon's menu. A new door into stringing reads `useStringingHidden` too.
- **The stock audit (2026-11-15, `docs/plans/string-stock-audit-fixes.md`; Grant: "fix 1-4 in one PR").** (1) **A job carrying `ownString: true` uses no set** — the player brought the string. Written by the request sheet's "I have my own", the bench's new-job checkbox (ticked by tapping the "Own string" price chip) and the job page's "String from" row (admin only: a stringer's PATCH still allows `status` alone); `true` or absent, never `false`; `usesASet` is false for it, so deleting one leaves no `stringUse`; both stock reads project `c.ownString`, and `toStringerJob` carries it so the stringer takes the player's string. Older jobs carry nothing and count as the club's. (2) **Strung past every metre logged is `overdrawnSets`** on the line and in `totals` (whole sets, and only once a purchase exists), shown on the shelf card and sheet, never clamped away in silence. (3) The colour pools above. (4) **Cost per set is the latest PAID REEL's price**, else the latest paid purchase, else the latest (all free reads $0), so a single retail set or a gifted reel no longer re-prices every set.
- **A restring can be INVOICED, and then it owes nothing until it is** (`docs/plans/stringing-invoice.md`, 2026-11-15; Grant: "For stringing jobs can we have sent invoice feature", then "Stringer taps it" and "Stringer, organiser"). Behind `NEXT_PUBLIC_FLAG_STRINGING_INVOICE` (server-read at CREATION only): a job made while it is on carries `billOn: 'invoice'` (`newJobBilling()` in both create routes), and is owed only once `invoice.sentAt` exists; every other job bills at Ready as before, so nobody's balance moved on deploy and the flag going off strands nobody. **`invoiceGateOpen` in `lib/stringingBilling.ts` is the ONE reading** — `isBillable`, the ledger mirror's `billableShaped` and the backfill's `jobEntries` all call it; a fourth "is this owed" that forgets it would bill a finished job before its invoice. `isOpenStringingJob` uses `isFinishedUnpaid` instead (a job not yet invoiced still keeps the club's stringing on), and the bench shows "Not invoiced · $X" (`awaitingInvoice`) on the live list and the archive, so un-invoiced money never goes quietly out of sight. `POST /api/stringing/jobs/[id]/invoice { memberId }` sends or resends: an admin or organiser of this club, or the stringer the job is ASSIGNED to (`canStringIn` + `stringerId`, re-checked like the status write); a finished, priced, unpaid, not-withdrawn job only (`invoiceRefusal`); the invoice is written under the job's etag FIRST, the mirror runs on it, then `deliverInvoice` (never throws) pushes WITHOUT an amount and emails WITH one (a walk-up only by email); a second tap inside a minute is 409 `sending`. The invoice number is the job's (`INV-0006`); `sentAt` never moves, a resend moves `lastSentAt` and clears the reminder stamps. **Reminders** (`lib/stringingInvoiceRemind.ts`, on the hourly tick): day 3 after the latest send, the player once; day 7, whoever sent it once (an organiser if they left the roster); each stamped on the invoice under the etag before the push, like the request nudge. A stringer who is not an admin sees a price in ONE place: `invoice` on their OWN queue (`toStringerJob(job, { contact: true })`), for a finished or invoiced job. The UI is one `components/stringing/StringingInvoiceCard.tsx` on the bench's job page and (`bare`) inside a stringer's job card; when nothing reached the person it opens the invoice picture (`StringingReceiptSheet asInvoice`).
- **The usage rate** (`docs/plans/string-usage-rate.md`, 2026-11-15; Grant: "option 2 tracks the usage rate", "Usage rate first"). `summarizeStringStock({ now })` adds `setsPerWeek`, `weeksLeft` and `reorder` to each line: sets strung a week over the last `RATE_WINDOW_WEEKS` (8), or since the club's FIRST strung job when that is sooner, never under a week (the mixed-window rule). A job counts on the day its history first says `strung` (`strungAtOf`), never when it was made; an own-string job counts nothing. `reorder` uses the shuttles' lines (under 2 weeks `now`, under 4 `soon`); no purchase or no rate is `null`, never "plenty". **Every stock read projects `STOCK_JOB_SELECT` and folds rows through `countedJob`** (`lib/stringStock.ts`) — a reader that drops `history` has no rate, one that drops `ownString` miscounts — and a deleted job's `stringUse` row carries `strungAt` (older rows count in the stock only). The club report carries the shelf as `stringShelf` (`lib/stringStockReport.ts`, attached by `clubMetrics()`): per string sets left, overdraw, rate, weeks left, reorder and cost per set, plus jobs by stage, archived and own-string — labels and counts, never a name; `null` when the club does not string. The photo stocktake that follows is `docs/plans/string-photo-stocktake.md` (intent).
- **The stocktake** (`docs/plans/string-photo-stocktake.md`, 2026-11-16; Grant: "taking a picture in app gives the stocking inventory", part reels "fullness by eye and weigh it", "Admins and stringers"). A count is a `kind: 'stringCount'` row in `clubSettings` (`lib/stringStocktake.ts`); **the latest count of a string RESETS it** in `summarizeStringStock({ counts })`: its sets become the pools' starting metres (by colour when the line names one), and only purchases created after it and jobs whose `strungAt` is after it move the shelf (a job with no date counts as before). `usedSets`, cost and the rate still count every job; `lastCount` is on the line, and a counted string is known stock even with no purchase (`stockBand`, `stringReorder`, the bench's `hasStock`). **Every stock reader goes through `loadStringStock`** (offered, purchases, catalog, `STOCK_JOB_SELECT` jobs, deleted uses, counts) — the stock route, `readShelfExtras` and the report — so a new input is added once. `POST /api/stringing/stocktake` re-adds every line on the SERVER (`validateCount`: packets + reels by eye or weight, offered labels only), `GET` gives the sheet sets and sizes and the last weigh-in, never money; both for an admin or this club's stringer (`stocktakeCaller`). `POST /api/stringing/stocktake/scan` sends one photo (≤ `MAX_PHOTO_BASE64`, shrunk by `lib/photoDownscale.ts`) and the shelf list to `STOCKTAKE_MODEL` with a fixed reply shape, maps labels to this shelf or `null`, saves nothing and stores no photo; 503 `ai_unavailable` without a key, and the sheet then counts by hand. A string the photo did not show is left OUT of the count, never zeroed. UI: `components/stringing/StocktakeSheet.tsx`, opened from the bench's shelf card and the stringer's.
- **The code review of 2026-11-16.** (1) **A job's number comes from a counter, never a count**: `nextJobSequence` in `lib/stringingJobNo.ts`, one `kind: 'stringingJobSeq'` doc per club in `clubSettings` (`jobSeqDocId(g)`), raised under its etag and seeded on first use from the highest `J-` number the club's jobs carry. "Count + 1" handed a deleted job's number to the next one and let two creates share one, and the invoice number is the job's. Both create routes call it. (2) **`PATCH /api/stringing/jobs/[id]` answers a non-admin stringer through `toStringerJob(job, { contact: true })`**, as the invoice route does; the raw job carried the price. (3) **The shelf list (`PATCH /api/stringing/strings`) writes under the etag of a raw read taken FIRST** and answers 409 `changed_elsewhere` when another save landed between, never `upsert`. (4) **A payment withdraws a price-changing `pendingEdit`** (the PATCH's `paid: true`, and `accept` on a job paid elsewhere, which then answers `job_paid` once), since a paid bill is never re-priced. The screens read the stringer queue, the shelf list and the prices through `sharedFetch`, and the stringer card and the bench take the PATCH answer in place of a full reload; a component test that leaves a request unanswered calls `resetSharedReads()` before each case.
